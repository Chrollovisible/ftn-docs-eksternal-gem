# Brand Onboarding

## How to get started

1. Review the [Service Plans](./paket-layanan.md) and choose the one that fits.
2. For the Starter plan, use the **Order Now** button on the website. For the Custom plan, use **Consult Now**.
3. For initial questions, use **Contact Us** on the website.
4. The Flagshiiip team will follow up on your brand's needs.

## What brands should prepare

- The brand and product names to be protected
- An estimate of the number of stickers needed
- For the Custom plan: needs for design, branding, and the loyalty program

The official onboarding flow after ordering (product registration, sticker production and delivery, and dashboard access) will be added once confirmed by the team.
