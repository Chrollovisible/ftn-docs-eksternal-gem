# For Brands

Are you a brand owner who wants to protect your products from counterfeiting? Flagshiiip provides stickers with a unique QR code to place on your product packaging, along with a dashboard to monitor scan activity.

Information about service plans and how to order is available at [flagshiiip.com](https://flagshiiip.com). You can also use the **Contact Us** button on that site to talk to the Flagshiiip team.
