# Quick Start

Flagshiiip helps you make sure the product you bought is truly authentic. It is easy and all you need is your phone.

![Buyer flow: get the app, scan the QR, see the result, claim a reward](/img/diagram/alur-pembeli-en.svg)

## Three steps

1. **Get the app.** Search for **FTN Apps** or **Flagshiiip** on Google Play or the App Store, then create an account.
2. **Scan the QR.** Point the app's camera at the Flagshiiip sticker on the product packaging.
3. **See the result.** If the product is authentic, a certificate of authenticity appears on your screen.

Some products also give a reward after scanning. If available, the app will show you how to claim it.
