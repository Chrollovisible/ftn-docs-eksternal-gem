# Blockchain, Explained Simply

You **do not need to understand this page** to use Flagshiiip. It is for readers who are curious about the technology behind the scenes.

## Imagine a shared notebook

Imagine a notebook with copies held by many people at once. Every time a new note is added, all the copies are updated. If someone secretly changes a note in their own copy, everyone else's copies will show the difference, so the change gets noticed.

That is the basic idea of a **blockchain**: a digital notebook stored in many places at once, which makes it hard to change in secret.

![A shared notebook copied in many places](/img/diagram/buku-catatan-en.svg)

## What does this have to do with Flagshiiip?

Flagshiiip uses this technology to support the following:

- **Product authenticity**: your scan result is backed by records that are hard to change.
- **Digital certificate of authenticity**: proof of authenticity you can see in the app.
- **Product ownership records**.
- **Loyalty rewards** in the form of tokens.

## What do you see as a user?

Almost nothing. You just scan the sticker and see the result. All the recording happens automatically behind the scenes.
