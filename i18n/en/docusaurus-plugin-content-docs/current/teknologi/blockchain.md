# Blockchain at Flagshiiip

## What is blockchain?

A blockchain is a digital ledger stored on many computers at once. Data that has been recorded is hard to change, so the records in it can be trusted without relying on a single party.

## The role of blockchain at Flagshiiip

Flagshiiip uses blockchain technology to:

- support **product authenticity verification**,
- support **digital certificates of authenticity**,
- record product **ownership**,
- run a **token-based loyalty program**.

For users, all of this looks simple: scan the QR, view the result, and claim tokens. The blockchain process runs behind the scenes.

See also [Security and Anti-Duplication](./keamanan-dan-anti-duplikasi.md) and [FTN Token](../token/token-ftn.md).
