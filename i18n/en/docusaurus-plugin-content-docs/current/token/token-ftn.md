# FTN Token

## Flagshiiip Tokenium

Flagshiiip Tokenium is a tokenization ecosystem focused on loyalty, turning transactions into valuable digital assets.

## Why a token?

Conventional loyalty programs often lose value quickly, are locked into a single brand, and are less flexible for their users. Flagshiiip Tokenium turns loyalty rewards into blockchain-based digital assets that can be transferred and last over time.

## The token's role in the ecosystem

- Serving as a loyalty reward for customers.
- Representing value in a blockchain-based loyalty program.

See also [Loyalty Program](./program-loyalitas.md) and [Tokenomics](./tokenomics.md).
