# Troubleshooting

## The QR will not scan

- Scan in a brighter place.
- Clean your camera lens.
- Move the phone closer or farther until the code is clear on the screen.
- Make sure the sticker is not damaged or covered.

## I cannot log in to my account

- Check your email and password again.
- Make sure your internet connection is stable.
- If it still fails, contact Flagshiiip support.

## The scan result is not verified

See the explanation in [Authenticity Verification](../cara-kerja/verifikasi-keaslian.md), then contact the seller or the brand.

## My wallet will not connect

- Make sure you are using a wallet the app supports.
- Close the app and open it again.
- If it still fails, contact Flagshiiip support.
