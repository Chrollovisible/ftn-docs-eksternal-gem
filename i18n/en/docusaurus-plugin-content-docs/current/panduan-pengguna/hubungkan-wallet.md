# Connect a Wallet

A wallet is a digital purse for storing tokens. In the Flagshiiip app, you can **connect an existing wallet** or **create a new wallet address**.

## Steps

1. Open the wallet settings in the app.
2. Choose to connect an existing wallet or create a new one.
3. Follow the on-screen instructions until the wallet address appears in the app.

## Wallet security

:::warning
Never share your wallet's **seed phrase** or **private key** with anyone, including people who claim to be from the Flagshiiip team. Anyone who holds that information can take everything in the wallet.
:::

- Store your seed phrase offline in a safe place.
- Do not keep it in screenshots or messages.

:::caution[Needs team verification]
The supported wallet types, the blockchain network used, and the exact steps in the app need to be confirmed.
:::
