# Scan the QR

## Steps

1. Make sure you are logged in to the app.
2. Choose the scanning feature in the app.
3. Allow the app to access the camera if asked.
4. Point the camera at the QR code on the Flagshiiip sticker on the product packaging.
5. Wait for the verification result to appear.

## Reading the result

An authentic product displays a **digital certificate of authenticity**. The product status is shown as authentic or verified.

## Tips

- Scan in a place with enough light.
- Make sure the sticker is not damaged or covered.
- Each QR code can only be claimed once. If the result shows the QR has already been claimed, contact the seller or the brand concerned.
