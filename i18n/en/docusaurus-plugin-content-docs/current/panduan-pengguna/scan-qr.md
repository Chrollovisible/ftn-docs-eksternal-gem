# Scan the QR

## Steps

1. Open the app and make sure you are logged in.
2. Choose the scanning feature.
3. If asked, allow the app to use the camera.
4. Point the camera at the QR code on the Flagshiiip sticker on the packaging.
5. Wait a few seconds until the result appears.

How to read the result is in [Authenticity Verification](../cara-kerja/verifikasi-keaslian.md).

## Tips for easy scanning

- Scan in a well-lit place.
- Hold your phone steady.
- Make sure the sticker is not dirty, damaged, or covered.
