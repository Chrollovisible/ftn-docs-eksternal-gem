# Claim a Reward

For certain products, the brand gives a reward to buyers who have verified the product. The reward comes as a **token**, a kind of digital point kept in your wallet.

## Steps

1. Scan the product's QR and make sure the result is verified.
2. [Connect your wallet](./hubungkan-wallet.md) in the app.
3. If the product gives a reward, the app will show you how to claim it. Follow the instructions.

Each QR code can only be claimed once. Want to know more about tokens? Read [What is a Token?](../token/token-ftn.md).
